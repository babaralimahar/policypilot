# IT equipment

## Laptop refresh

We provide staff with reliable technology to ensure that work remains efficient and productive. To maintain high performance standards, our company initiates a laptop refresh every 3 years. This cycle helps our team avoid technical delays and ensures that everyone possesses hardware capable of handling modern software requirements. Please contact the help desk if you experience persistent issues with your current device before your scheduled upgrade arrives.

When your refresh cycle concludes, the hardware team will reach out to schedule a convenient swap. You must back up all essential documents to the secure cloud storage before returning your old equipment to us. Our technicians will then assist with transferring your data to the replacement machine. We appreciate your cooperation in keeping our infrastructure current and your commitment to safeguarding company information throughout this transition.

## Personal devices

We encourage you to use your own equipment for work tasks when you are away from the office. To keep our internal data safe, we ask that you add a device management profile to your phone or laptop. This tool helps us protect information without accessing your private photos or messages. We want you to feel comfortable staying connected while keeping our digital workspace secure and efficient for the entire team.

If you choose to work from your own hardware, please remember that you must use company email only for professional correspondence. Mixing personal accounts with work matters creates security risks that we need to avoid. Please reach out to the tech team if you have questions about how to set this up correctly. We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining these standards as we continue to grow and support our clients across the globe.
