# Business travel

## Flights

When traveling for company business, please book economy class for flights under 6 hours to keep our expenses reasonable. We trust you to choose the most efficient routes available through our approved travel portal. Please select standard seating options whenever possible to help us maintain a balanced budget across the entire team.

If your travel needs require a different cabin or special seating arrangements, you may request an upgrade with director approval. Make sure to submit these requests well ahead of your departure so that we have sufficient time to review your specific situation. We appreciate your cooperation in managing travel resources wisely while you represent Northwind Supplies on the road.

## Hotels

We want you to stay comfortable while representing Northwind Supplies during your trips. Please book standard rooms at reputable hotels that fit within the travel budget guidelines provided for your destination. When traveling to locations where the primary currency is the dollar, your limit is USD 200 per night. We appreciate your efforts to choose convenient locations that keep our company costs reasonable while ensuring you have a restful place to recharge.

For employees traveling to our regional offices where the currency is the rupee, the limit is PKR 25,000 per night. If you find that local rates exceed these amounts due to conferences or special events, please contact your manager for approval before finalizing your booking. We trust your judgment in selecting accommodations that align with our professional standards. Thank you for your diligence in managing these expenses and for your hard work on behalf of our team.
