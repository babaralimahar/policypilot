# Compensation guide for managers

## Salary bands

At Northwind Supplies, we strive to provide competitive pay that reflects the vital leadership roles our managers fill. For positions based within our primary regional offices, the annual base pay range is set at USD 110,000 to 140,000. These figures ensure that our compensation remains equitable while supporting the diverse needs of our growing team members across the globe.

We also maintain dedicated pay structures for our international locations to account for local market conditions and living costs. For our regional management roles situated abroad, the designated salary range is PKR 9,000,000 to 12,000,000. We encourage managers to reach out to the human resources department if they have questions regarding how their specific pay is determined or if they wish to discuss the broader benefits package available to staff. Our goal is to maintain transparency and fairness in all rewards programs throughout the company.

## Merit budget

The company provides a merit budget to support your efforts in rewarding high performance within your departments. Each manager receives an allocation equivalent to 3.5% of team payroll to distribute among eligible staff based on individual contributions and goal achievement. We trust you to make thoughtful decisions that align with the professional growth of your team members while maintaining fair standards across the entire organization.

Please evaluate your staff carefully to ensure that salary adjustments reflect their dedication and specific impact throughout the year. You should discuss your final recommendations with human resources to confirm alignment with our internal guidelines before sharing any updates with your direct reports. We appreciate your diligent work in managing these resources to keep our workforce engaged and motivated as we continue striving toward our collective goals together at Northwind Supplies.

## Manager bonus

Northwind Supplies values the dedication that our leaders show in driving company success. As a token of appreciation for your commitment to our collective goals, we offer an annual incentive program designed to reward exceptional performance. If specific departmental objectives are reached, eligible leaders receive a bonus equal to 15% of base salary. This payout recognizes the effort put forth to guide teams toward excellence while maintaining our high standards of service.

We encourage managers to maintain open communication regarding their progress toward these targets throughout the year. Your supervisor will schedule meetings to discuss milestones and provide feedback to help you succeed in this role. Please reach out to the human resources team if you have questions about the eligibility criteria or the evaluation process. We are proud to have you on our team and thank you for your hard work in helping our business reach new heights.
