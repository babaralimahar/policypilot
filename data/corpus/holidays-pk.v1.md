# Public holidays (Pakistan)

## 2026 holiday calendar

Northwind Supplies observes 13 public holidays in 2026 to ensure our team members enjoy quality time with their families and friends. We believe that taking breaks is essential for maintaining a healthy work-life balance and keeping spirits high throughout the year. Our office remains closed during these designated periods so everyone can participate in cultural festivities and regional traditions without any professional distractions.

We are proud to honor local customs and provide specific leave for major observances. Employees receive three days for Eid ul-Fitr to celebrate this important occasion with loved ones. Please check the main breakroom notice board or reach out to your direct manager if you need clarification regarding specific office closures or scheduling procedures for the remainder of the year. We value your commitment to the team and encourage you to plan your personal leave around these observed breaks to make the most of your time away from the office.

## Working on a public holiday

We understand that certain operational needs may occasionally require staff to report for duty during a public holiday in Pakistan. If you are requested to work on such a day, we deeply value your dedication and flexibility in supporting our business continuity. Please ensure that your manager provides prior approval before you work, as this helps us manage our teams effectively while respecting the importance of your downtime.

To make sure you get the rest you deserve, our policy allows you to take a compensatory day off within 30 days of the worked holiday. You should coordinate with your supervisor to select a suitable time that minimizes disruption to your team. We encourage you to plan this break promptly to maintain a healthy work-life balance. Thank you for your continued hard work and commitment to our collective success at Northwind Supplies.
