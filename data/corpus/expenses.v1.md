# Expenses policy

## Meals while travelling

When you travel for Northwind Supplies, we want you to stay nourished and comfortable. You may claim reimbursement for food consumed during your business trips up to a total of USD 40 per day. Please remember to keep all of your receipts so that the finance team can process your requests without delay. We encourage you to choose healthy options that help you perform your best while you are away representing our company.

If you have questions about specific items or need assistance with the submission portal, please reach out to the office manager for support. We strive to make your travel experience smooth and stress free. Your well being is important to us, so do not hesitate to ask if you require clarification regarding these guidelines. We appreciate all the hard work you put into your assignments and look forward to hearing about your successful journeys. Thank you for being a valued part of our team and for your cooperation with these requirements.
