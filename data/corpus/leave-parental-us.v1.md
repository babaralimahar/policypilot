# Parental leave (United States)

## Primary caregiver leave

We support you as you welcome a new addition to your family. At Northwind Supplies, we believe that bonding time is essential for every parent. Eligible staff members serving as the primary caregiver are entitled to 14 weeks of paid leave to focus on their family during this special transition. This time away is designed to ensure you feel rested and ready for your return to our team.

Please coordinate with your manager and the human resources department well in advance to arrange your absence. We ask that you provide notice so your colleagues can prepare for your brief departure. Our goal is to make your transition into parenthood smooth and stress-free. We value your contributions to our company and look forward to celebrating your expanding family with you. Thank you for all that you do to make our workplace a wonderful environment.

## Secondary caregiver leave

Northwind Supplies supports your growing family by offering secondary caregiver leave to eligible employees. We recognize the importance of bonding with a child and assisting with household adjustments during this transition. You are eligible for 4 weeks of paid leave to spend precious moments with your new arrival. This time away allows you to support your partner and settle into your new routines without the pressure of work responsibilities.

To request this time, please submit a formal notification to your manager and the human resources department. We encourage you to plan your absence well in advance to ensure a smooth transition for your colleagues and your team. Your commitment to your family remains a priority for us, and we are pleased to provide this benefit as part of our comprehensive support package. We look forward to your return after you have completed your leave, refreshed and ready to contribute to our collective success once again.

## Eligibility

Northwind Supplies supports your growing family and wants to ensure you have the time needed to bond with your child. All team members who meet the requirements become eligible for this benefit after 90 days of employment. We encourage you to plan ahead and discuss your upcoming leave with your supervisor as soon as you feel comfortable sharing the news.

Our human resources department remains available to guide you through the process of requesting your time away. We aim to make this transition as smooth as possible for you and your household. Please reach out to the benefits team if you have questions regarding your status or if you need assistance submitting the necessary paperwork for your request. We are proud to foster a workplace culture that values the personal lives of our dedicated staff members.
