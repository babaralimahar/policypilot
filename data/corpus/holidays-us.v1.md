# Company holidays (United States)

## 2026 holiday calendar

Northwind Supplies recognizes that our team thrives when everyone has time to recharge and connect with loved ones. To support your wellbeing, we observe 11 paid holidays in 2026. These occasions provide you with scheduled opportunities to step away from your daily responsibilities and celebrate throughout the calendar period. We believe that balancing hard work with rest is essential to our culture and collective success as a company.

The calendar reflects our commitment to honoring traditional observances while ensuring that our operations remain consistent for our clients. Please plan your personal schedule accordingly to enjoy these gatherings. If you have questions regarding your specific team requirements during these closures, feel free to reach out to your manager. We are proud to offer this time off as a gesture of appreciation for the dedication and expertise you bring to the office every single day.

## Floating holiday

Northwind Supplies values the diverse traditions and personal commitments of every team member. To support this balance, we grant all eligible staff members one floating holiday per year to use at their discretion. This day is intended to offer flexibility for religious observances, cultural celebrations, or personal errands that fall outside of our standard company closure schedule.

Please coordinate with your manager well before the day you plan to be away from the office. Since we strive to maintain consistent service levels for our clients, we ask that you verify team coverage prior to requesting your leave. Once approved, you are free to enjoy your time off as you see fit. We believe this benefit enhances our workplace culture and helps everyone stay refreshed while contributing to our shared goals.
