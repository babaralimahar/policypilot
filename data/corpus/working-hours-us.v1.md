# Working hours (United States)

## Core hours

To support our collaborative culture, Northwind Supplies maintains set windows where team members across the country are available for meetings and direct communication. We ask that everyone remain accessible during the block of 10:00 to 15:00 Central Time. This window helps us align our various efforts while ensuring that colleagues can reach each other for urgent tasks or shared projects without significant delays.

Outside of these designated hours, you have the flexibility to structure your personal schedule in a way that best supports your individual productivity and balance. We trust our staff to manage their responsibilities effectively while staying present during our shared period. Please coordinate with your manager if you anticipate any recurring needs that might impact your availability during this span. We appreciate your dedication to keeping our communication seamless and efficient as we grow together.

## Overtime

We appreciate your hard work and dedication to the team. To ensure everyone stays balanced, we strive to keep schedules predictable for all staff members. If your position is eligible for extra compensation, you will receive 1.5 times the regular rate after 40 hours of labor during a workweek. Please confirm your specific eligibility status with your manager or the human resources department before logging additional effort.

We ask that you obtain approval from your supervisor before working beyond your scheduled shift. This process helps our leaders manage project loads effectively and keeps our operations running smoothly. If you have questions about recording your time accurately, our payroll team is available to assist you with the digital tracking system. Thank you for your commitment to excellence as we grow together at Northwind Supplies.
