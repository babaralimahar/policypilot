# Code of conduct

## Gifts and hospitality

At Northwind Supplies, we value professional integrity in all our business relationships. We recognize that occasional tokens of appreciation from partners can strengthen our connections. You may accept modest gifts that are worth up to USD 50 provided they do not influence your decision-making or create a sense of obligation. Please ensure that any items received are appropriate for a workplace setting and reflect positively on our company reputation.

We also encourage you to exercise sound judgment regarding hospitality. If a meal or event exceeds the standard expectations of professional courtesy, please discuss the situation with your manager before attending. Transparency is key to maintaining trust with our clients and vendors. By keeping these exchanges reasonable and infrequent, we uphold our commitment to fairness. Thank you for your dedication to keeping our standards high and our interactions honest as we continue to grow together.

## Conflicts of interest

At Northwind Supplies, we value the trust you build with our partners and community. A conflict of interest happens when your personal activities or outside relationships interfere with your ability to make objective decisions for our company. We ask that you avoid situations where your private interests clash with your professional duties. Please keep your work responsibilities separate from any external business ventures or personal financial gains.

Transparency remains the best way to handle these concerns. If you find yourself in a situation that could be seen as a conflict, speak with your manager immediately. You must submit a written disclosure regarding the potential issue within 14 days of realizing that a conflict exists. This process helps us resolve concerns early while protecting your reputation and the integrity of our team. We are here to support you in maintaining professional standards while you grow your career with us.

## Speaking up

At Northwind Supplies, we believe that an open workplace is built on trust and integrity. We encourage every team member to share concerns about unethical actions or potential policy violations without fear of retaliation. If you notice something that does not seem right, please speak with your direct supervisor or a member of the people team. We promise to listen carefully and handle your report with strict confidentiality to ensure that our office remains a safe and fair environment for everyone.

If you prefer to report an issue anonymously or need additional guidance, you can call our dedicated support line at 0800-NORTH. This resource is available to all staff members seeking to clarify expectations or discuss matters that affect our shared culture. By coming forward, you help us maintain the high standards that define our company and support the success of your colleagues. We appreciate your commitment to keeping our workplace professional, transparent, and supportive for each person who contributes to our mission.
