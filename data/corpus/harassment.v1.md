# Anti-harassment

## How to report

If you experience or witness behavior that makes you feel uncomfortable, we encourage you to speak up right away. Our culture depends on mutual respect, so please share your concerns by reaching out to your HR partner or the ethics hotline at any point. We treat every report with seriousness and care to ensure that our workplace remains safe and inclusive for everyone.

Once you reach out, we will listen to your account and start a thorough review of the situation. We are committed to protecting your privacy throughout the process and will take appropriate action to resolve the matter. You are not alone in this, and our dedicated team is here to support you whenever you need guidance. We value your voice and appreciate your efforts in helping us maintain the positive environment that defines our company.

## Investigations

If you experience or observe behavior that violates our workplace standards, please report the matter to your supervisor or the human resources department immediately. We take every concern seriously and remain committed to fostering a respectful environment for everyone. Upon receiving a formal report, we will initiate a thorough review to gather facts and speak with involved parties. Our team promises to acknowledge your submission within 5 working days to ensure you feel heard and supported throughout the process.

We handle all reports with the highest level of care and sensitivity to protect the privacy of those involved. During the inquiry, we ask that you cooperate fully so that we may reach a fair resolution. Any employee found to be engaging in prohibited conduct will face appropriate corrective action. We encourage open communication and want you to feel comfortable raising issues without fear of retaliation. Our goal is to maintain a professional workplace where every person feels safe, valued, and empowered to succeed in their role at Northwind Supplies.

## No retaliation

At Northwind Supplies, we believe that every team member deserves a workplace built on respect and support. If you report harassment or participate in an investigation, you are protected from any form of negative treatment. We strictly prohibit any action that penalizes you for speaking up. We want you to feel comfortable sharing concerns, knowing that your voice is valued and your well-being remains our priority.

Any person who attempts to intimidate or harm a colleague for reporting an issue will face immediate disciplinary action. We take this commitment seriously because we want our culture to stay positive and safe for everyone. Retaliating against a coworker in this manner is grounds for dismissal. Please reach out to your manager or the human resources department if you feel that your rights under this policy are not being upheld or if you have questions about how we handle these sensitive situations together.
