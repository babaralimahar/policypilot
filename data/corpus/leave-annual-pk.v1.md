# Annual leave (Pakistan)

## Entitlement

We are pleased to provide all eligible staff members with 20 working days of annual leave each year. This benefit is designed to ensure that everyone has sufficient time away from the office to rest, recharge, and enjoy personal pursuits. We believe that taking regular breaks is essential for maintaining a healthy work-life balance and contributes to the long-term success and wellbeing of our entire team here at Northwind Supplies.

You will accrue your leave balance at a rate of 1.67 days per month throughout the calendar year. Please coordinate with your manager well ahead of your requested time off to ensure that your responsibilities are covered while you are away. We kindly ask that you submit your requests through our internal portal so that your balance remains accurate. We truly value your hard work and dedication, and we encourage you to utilize this time to step back and focus on your personal well-being.

## Carrying leave over

We understand that sometimes you might not be able to use all your vacation time during the current period. To help with this, you have the option of carrying over up to 10 days of unused leave into the next cycle. We want to ensure you get the rest you need while maintaining our high standards of service.

Please make sure you submit your request to your manager before 31 March so that we can update our records accordingly. Any leave that remains beyond this balance will be lost if not used, so we encourage you to plan ahead and enjoy your breaks throughout the year. Thank you for your hard work and commitment to our team.

## Booking leave

We encourage you to take regular breaks to recharge and spend time with family. To request annual leave, please submit a formal application through our internal portal at least two weeks in advance. This lead time helps your manager coordinate shift patterns and ensures that your teammates can support your responsibilities while you are away from the office.

Once you have entered your request, your supervisor will review the details to confirm that coverage is sufficient. We ask that you speak with your direct lead about your plans before finalizing any travel arrangements. Please be mindful of team project cycles when selecting your preferred getaway period, as this proactive approach keeps our operations running smoothly for everyone involved. Your wellness remains a priority, and we look forward to helping you manage your rest periods effectively throughout the cycle.
