# Sick leave and wellbeing days

## Paid sick days

At Northwind Supplies, we truly value your health and personal recovery. We provide 10 paid sick days per year to help you take care of yourself when you feel unwell. Please notify your manager as early as possible if you need to use this time so that your team can support your absence. Your wellbeing is our priority, and we encourage you to rest fully before returning to work.

If you face a prolonged health challenge, please reach out to our human resources team to discuss your situation. We aim to support a balanced environment where everyone feels comfortable focusing on their wellness. You should keep your supervisor informed about your status to ensure proper coverage for your tasks. We appreciate your dedication to maintaining a healthy workplace for all colleagues and look forward to having you back when you are ready.

## Medical certificates

We prioritize your health and wellness at Northwind Supplies. If you feel unwell, please take the necessary time to recover and return to work feeling refreshed. We trust our team members to manage their health responsibly, so you do not need to provide formal documentation for brief absences. This approach helps reduce administrative burdens for everyone while supporting a culture of mutual trust.

We only require a medical certificate if you are absent for more than 3 consecutive days. Should this happen, please ask your healthcare provider for a document confirming your inability to work. You can then submit this paperwork to your manager upon your return to the office. This process ensures we can provide appropriate support while you are away. We hope you stay healthy and feel comfortable using your leave whenever your wellbeing requires it.

## Wellbeing days

At Northwind Supplies, we truly value the health of our staff and recognize that everyone needs a brief pause to recharge. You are eligible for 2 wellbeing days per year to focus on your personal balance or mental health. Please coordinate with your supervisor ahead of your planned break so that your department can manage the workflow seamlessly while you step away.

These breaks are meant to provide you with space for relaxation and self-care. We encourage you to use this period to disconnect fully from your professional tasks and return feeling restored. Your contribution is vital to our team, and we hope these occasions help you maintain a positive outlook during your work. Please reach out to your lead if you have questions about scheduling these leave requests.
