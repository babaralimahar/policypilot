# Parental leave (Pakistan)

## Maternity leave

We are thrilled to support growing families at Northwind Supplies by providing 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave to eligible employees. This period is intended to help you focus on your health and bond with your new addition to the household. Please reach out to your manager and the human resources team as early as possible so we can coordinate your time away and ensure a smooth transition of your responsibilities.

We want you to feel comfortable and confident during this transition. Your role is vital to our mission, and we are committed to making your return to the office as seamless as possible. Should you have questions about administrative tasks or local requirements regarding this benefit, our personnel department stands ready to assist you. We celebrate this joyful chapter in your life and look forward to welcoming you back whenever you are ready to rejoin the team.

## Paternity leave

We are delighted to support new fathers as they welcome a child into their family. At Northwind Supplies, we recognize the importance of this special time for bonding and adjusting to life at home. To assist you during this period, all eligible employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave. We encourage you to use this time to care for your newborn and support your partner as you navigate this transition together.

Please coordinate with your manager well in advance to ensure that your responsibilities are covered while you are away. We aim to make the request process as smooth as possible for every team member. Our goal is to ensure you feel fully supported by the company while you celebrate your growing family. We wish you and your loved ones the very best during this wonderful chapter of your life.

## Adoption leave

At Northwind Supplies, we embrace your journey into parenthood with full support. If you welcome a child into your family through legal adoption, we offer leave to help you bond and settle in at home. This time away from the office is intended to provide a stable foundation for your child and support your transition during this life event. We are delighted to share in your joy as you grow your family.

You are eligible for 12 weeks for the primary caregiver upon the arrival of your child. Please reach out to your manager and the human resources team as early as you are able to discuss your plans and ensure a smooth handover of your current tasks. We want to make sure you have complete peace of mind while you are away. Our team remains committed to fostering an inclusive workplace where every parent feels valued and respected. We look forward to supporting you throughout this important experience.
