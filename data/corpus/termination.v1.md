# Ending employment (manager guide)

## Notice periods

When a team member decides to leave Northwind Supplies, please ensure they provide formal notification in writing. We ask that you verify their contractual agreement regarding the expected departure timeline to ensure a smooth transition of responsibilities. We require 90 days for managers to allow for a thorough handover and the selection of a successor. This standard helps maintain stability across our departments and ensures that ongoing projects continue without disruption.

Please coordinate closely with the human resources team as soon as you receive a resignation. They will assist you in preparing the necessary final documentation and support the departing staff member during their final stretch with the company. Your support during this phase is vital to our collective success and positive culture. We appreciate your dedication to managing these transitions with professionalism, kindness, and clear communication to keep our workplace functioning at its best for everyone involved.

## Severance

When an employee leaves the company under qualifying circumstances, we offer a severance package to support their transition. This benefit is calculated based on 2 weeks of base pay per year of service. We aim to ensure that our departing team members feel respected and valued for the dedication they showed during their time with us. Please coordinate with the human resources department to confirm eligibility before discussing these details with the staff member.

Our policy ensures fairness across the organization while remaining financially sustainable for the business. This total benefit is capped at 26 weeks for all eligible individuals. We trust that you will approach these sensitive conversations with empathy, professionalism, and clarity. Should you have questions about applying this approach to a specific situation, reach out to your partner in human resources for guidance. We appreciate your commitment to handling these difficult departures with the kindness that defines the culture at Northwind Supplies.
