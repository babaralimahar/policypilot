# Onboarding

## Before your first day

We are thrilled to welcome you to the team. To ensure everything is ready for your arrival, please complete your digital paperwork at least 5 working days before your start date. This step allows our staff to set up your account and prepare your workspace so that you feel right at home immediately. Please reach out to our office manager if you encounter any trouble while accessing the portal.

Once your information is confirmed, we will email your login credentials and a map showing the best spot for parking. Please review the attached guide that explains our office culture and local amenities. We look forward to meeting you soon and getting started on our upcoming projects together. Your presence helps us continue providing excellent service to our clients, and we are glad that you decided to join our organization for this exciting new chapter.

## Onboarding buddy

To help you settle into the team, we pair every new starter with an onboarding buddy for your first 90 days. Your buddy is a seasoned team member who acts as a friendly guide to our culture and workspace. They are there to answer questions about internal processes, help you find necessary resources, and introduce you to colleagues across various departments. This relationship is designed to make your transition smooth and welcoming as you get settled.

Please feel free to reach out to your buddy whenever you need clarification or informal support. They can assist with navigation of our systems, share tips on local perks, or simply grab a coffee to catch up. By establishing this connection, we aim to ensure that you feel supported, valued, and comfortable from the moment you join us. We encourage open communication and active participation during this period to help you build a strong foundation for a successful tenure here.

## Probation

We welcome you to the team and hope your transition into Northwind Supplies is smooth and productive. Every new hire completes a formal assessment period to ensure our goals align. This vital phase lasts 3 months in Pakistan and 90 days in the United States to give everyone enough space to settle into their roles and demonstrate their potential. 

During this evaluation, your manager will provide regular feedback to support your growth and development. We encourage you to ask questions and seek clarity on your tasks as you learn our processes. Successful completion of this period marks your transition into a permanent staff member. We truly value your contributions and look forward to seeing your skills flourish as you become a core part of our professional family.
