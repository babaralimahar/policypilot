# Expenses policy

## Meals while travelling

When you travel for Northwind Supplies, we want you to stay well fueled. You are welcome to claim reimbursement for your dining costs up to USD 60 per day. Please remember to keep all of your receipts for each meal you purchase, as our accounting department needs these documents to process your payments. You should submit your claims through the internal portal soon after you return to the office to ensure a smooth and prompt repayment process.

We trust you to select nutritious food that keeps you energized for your professional commitments. Please choose options that align with our company values regarding reasonable spending. If you encounter any issues while you are on the road, feel free to reach out to the support team for guidance. We appreciate your hard work and dedication while representing our brand during your trips. Thank you for your continued commitment to our shared success.

## Receipts

To keep our financial records accurate and help you get paid back quickly, please save all of your documentation for business purchases. We require a valid digital or paper copy of your receipt for any expense over USD 25 that you submit through our portal. These records allow our accounting department to verify transactions efficiently and ensure that everything aligns with our company guidelines.

If you lose a document for a purchase, please reach out to your manager to discuss alternative ways to verify the transaction. Having clear paperwork makes the reimbursement process smooth for everyone involved. If you have questions about whether a purchase qualifies for repayment, check with the finance team before you pay. We appreciate your diligence in managing these details as it helps us maintain transparency across Northwind Supplies while supporting your work goals.

## Submitting claims

To ensure that you are reimbursed promptly, please submit all business related claims through Expensify. We kindly ask that you attach clear digital copies of your receipts to every report so that our finance team can verify your entries without delay. This streamlined process helps us keep our records accurate and ensures that your funds reach your personal account as quickly as possible.

Please make sure to finalize your report within 30 days of the purchase date. By staying on top of your submissions, you help the department manage our collective budget effectively and avoid any potential processing holdups. If you ever encounter technical difficulties or have questions regarding a specific transaction, please reach out to the support staff. We appreciate your diligence in keeping our expense procedures running smoothly for the benefit of the entire team at Northwind Supplies.

## Approvals

All expense reports require review by your direct supervisor to ensure that every charge aligns with company goals. Please submit your documentation promptly after travel or purchases conclude to help us process reimbursements with efficiency. We value your careful attention to detail throughout this simple workflow.

Requests exceeding the standard spending limit above USD 1,000 also need Finance approval to verify budget compliance. Once your manager gives the signal, please forward the digital forms to the accounting team for their final assessment. Our staff remains happy to assist you with questions regarding this process to ensure that your claims move through the system without delay.
