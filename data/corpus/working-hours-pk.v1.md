# Working hours (Pakistan)

## Core hours

At Northwind Supplies, we embrace a flexible culture that supports your personal balance while ensuring our team remains connected and productive. To help us coordinate meetings and collaborative projects across our department, everyone must be available online during the core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 PKT. During this designated window, please make sure you are reachable via our internal messaging platform or email so we can support each other effectively.

Outside of the core period, you have the freedom to structure your remaining tasks in a way that suits your workflow best. We trust our staff to manage their responsibilities with care and professionalism. If you encounter any challenges regarding your schedule, please reach out to your manager to discuss adjustments. We value the dedication you bring to your role and appreciate your commitment to maintaining strong communication as we work together to achieve our goals.

## Friday prayers

We respect the religious observances of our team members and encourage everyone to manage their tasks effectively throughout the week. Employees who wish to attend congregational services may take a break from 13:00 to 14:30 each week. Please coordinate with your manager to ensure your team has sufficient coverage during this period.

We believe that supporting your personal commitments helps build a balanced and positive workplace. Kindly communicate your plans ahead of time so that we maintain our smooth operations. Our culture thrives when we accommodate these meaningful traditions while continuing to meet the goals that we set together. We appreciate your dedication to excellence and your professional cooperation regarding this schedule.
