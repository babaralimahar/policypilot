# Remote and hybrid work

## Hybrid schedule

We embrace a flexible culture at Northwind Supplies to help everyone balance professional goals with personal needs. Our hybrid schedule allows eligible staff to work from home up to 2 days per week while maintaining strong connections with colleagues in the office. This approach ensures we keep the collaborative spirit that defines our workplace while providing the autonomy you need to do your best work.

Please coordinate with your manager to select the specific days that align with team meetings and project requirements. We find that consistent patterns help teams stay synchronized and productive. By choosing a regular routine, you help us maintain a vibrant office environment where creative ideas flourish and support is always available. We appreciate your dedication to making our hybrid model successful as we continue growing together.

## Working from another country

We understand that many team members enjoy exploring different cultures while balancing professional responsibilities. If you wish to work from another country, you may do so for up to 30 days per year. Please keep in mind that this flexibility depends on your current tax status and the specific requirements of your role to ensure compliance with international regulations.

Before making any travel arrangements, you must secure written approval from HR. This ensures that our teams remain coordinated and that your remote workspace meets our security standards. We encourage you to discuss your plans with your manager well ahead of your departure to verify that your responsibilities are covered while you are away. We want to support your desire for flexibility while maintaining the high quality of work that defines Northwind Supplies.

## Home office equipment

We provide a one-time home office stipend of USD 300 to help you create a comfortable and productive workspace. You may use these funds for essential items such as an ergonomic chair, a desk, or professional lighting to support your daily tasks while you work from your house. Please keep your receipts and submit them through our expense portal for reimbursement after you purchase your selected equipment.

Our team wants to ensure that you have the tools necessary to succeed in your remote role. If you need specific technical accessories or peripheral devices to connect with your workstation, please reach out to the technology department for assistance. We aim to support your health and well-being by helping you maintain a functional setup that meets your personal requirements. Your manager is available to answer any questions you might have regarding the selection of your gear or the reimbursement process.
