# Hiring guide for managers

## Opening a role

Before you begin looking for a candidate, you must secure formal approval from your VP to ensure that the headcount aligns with our current business objectives. Reach out to your department lead to confirm that the budget supports a fresh addition to your team. Once you have that confirmation, you should draft a clear job description that highlights the core responsibilities and essential skills required for success in the position. We want to make sure that each recruit understands how they contribute to our mission at Northwind Supplies.

After finishing the job description, please send the draft to the human resources team for a brief review. They will help you refine the language to ensure it stays welcoming and inclusive for all applicants. The recruitment staff will then coordinate the posting process across our career channels so that we attract talented people who share our passion for excellence. We appreciate your efforts in building a strong team and thank you for following these steps to keep our hiring process organized and effective.

## Interview panel

To ensure a fair and balanced hiring process, every candidate must meet with at least 3 interviewers before we extend a job offer. This approach helps our team gather diverse perspectives on each applicant while reducing personal bias. Please select colleagues from different departments to join your panel, as this variety provides candidates with a better understanding of the collaborative culture here at Northwind Supplies.

Please coordinate schedules carefully so that all participants can be present for the entire discussion. We encourage you to share evaluation forms immediately after the meeting concludes so that feedback remains fresh and accurate. By working together as a group, we can make thoughtful hiring decisions that strengthen our company. We appreciate your dedication to finding great additions to our team and thank you for following this standardized process for every vacancy.

## Offers

When extending a formal offer to a potential team member, please coordinate closely with your dedicated human resources partner to ensure internal equity and alignment with our total rewards philosophy. We encourage you to present the complete package clearly, highlighting our commitment to professional growth and long-term success at Northwind Supplies. Be prepared to discuss the details of the compensation package with transparency and warmth to help the candidate feel welcomed into our collaborative culture.

Should you determine that a candidate possesses unique skills that justify a salary offer above the band midpoint, you must secure written approval from the department director and the talent acquisition lead prior to communication. This extra step helps us maintain consistency across all teams while still allowing the flexibility needed to attract top talent. We trust your judgment in identifying individuals who bring exceptional value to our mission and look forward to supporting your efforts in building a strong, inclusive workforce.
