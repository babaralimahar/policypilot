# Provident fund (Pakistan)

## Contributions

Northwind Supplies supports your long-term financial security by facilitating a mandatory provident fund scheme for all eligible staff members based in Pakistan. To participate in this arrangement, the company deducts 8.33% of basic salary from your monthly earnings to be deposited into your individual account. This disciplined approach helps you accumulate savings steadily while you remain part of our dedicated team, ensuring that you have resources available for your future needs.

The company demonstrates its commitment to your professional journey by matching the contribution amount remitted from your pay. These combined funds are managed with complete transparency to ensure that your retirement planning stays on track throughout your employment with us. We encourage every team member to review the plan details regularly to stay informed about how these collective efforts benefit your overall compensation package. Should you require further clarification regarding the administrative processes or the specific nature of these payments, please reach out to the human resources department for assistance.

## Vesting

We are delighted to offer a retirement savings plan to support your long term financial security as part of our team here in Pakistan. Your contributions to the provident fund are always yours to keep, but company contributions follow a specific schedule based on your tenure. You begin to earn a vested interest in the firm portion of the fund after 2 years of service. This transition happens automatically to ensure that you are building meaningful wealth as you continue to grow your career with us.

If you decide to move on from Northwind Supplies before hitting this milestone, you will receive your personal contributions plus all accrued interest. Any employer matches that have not reached the threshold for vesting will revert back to the general company fund. We encourage you to reach out to the human resources department if you have questions regarding your account status or how these balance calculations function. Our staff is ready to help you understand your benefits so you can plan for your future with complete confidence and peace of mind.
