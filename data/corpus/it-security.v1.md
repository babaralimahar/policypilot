# Information security

## Passwords

At Northwind Supplies, keeping company data safe is a shared duty. To protect our digital assets, please ensure that every login credential consists of at least 14 characters. We ask that you avoid using simple sequences or common phrases that might be easy for outsiders to guess. Mixing uppercase letters, lowercase letters, and symbols creates a robust barrier that helps us maintain the trust of our clients and partners.

For managing your credentials securely, please use 1Password. This tool removes the burden of remembering many complex strings by storing them safely in a single encrypted vault. You should never share your vault access or write down your keys in plain view. By relying on this approved manager, you contribute to a stronger defense for our entire organization. Your cooperation helps ensure that our systems remain secure and accessible to those who need them.

## Multi-factor authentication

We prioritize the safety of our internal systems and sensitive client data by keeping our digital assets protected at all times. To achieve this, multi-factor authentication is required on every company account that grants access to our servers or internal platforms. This simple step adds a vital layer of defense against unauthorized access and helps us maintain the trust of our partners. By requiring an additional verification step, we ensure that your identity is confirmed whenever you log in from different locations.

Please reach out to the technology team if you encounter any trouble while setting up your security preferences or if you lose access to your secondary verification device. Our staff is here to help you navigate the process so that we can keep our professional environment secure. We appreciate your cooperation in following these standard procedures as we work together to protect our collaborative workspace and shared information from potential threats. Your diligence in maintaining these security standards is essential for our collective success and the ongoing protection of our professional reputation.

## Lost or stolen devices

If you misplace your company laptop or mobile device, please notify the information security department at security@northwind.example immediately. Prompt reporting helps our team secure your accounts and protect sensitive company data from unauthorized access. We understand that accidents happen, and we are here to support you through the recovery process.

You must submit a formal incident report detailing the loss or theft within 24 hours of discovering the missing equipment. Please include a brief description of the circumstances surrounding the event so our staff can assist with remote wiping procedures or hardware replacement. Your cooperation is vital to maintaining our collective digital safety and ensures that Northwind Supplies remains protected against potential threats. Thank you for your commitment to keeping our information safe.

## Phishing

Phishing is a common way that attackers try to steal sensitive credentials by posing as legitimate entities. Please stay alert whenever you receive unexpected emails requesting that you click links, open attachments, or provide your login details. If an urgent request seems strange or suspicious, pause before taking action and verify the sender through a different communication channel. We want to ensure that every team member feels confident in identifying these deceptive messages to keep our company data safe from unauthorized access.

Should you encounter a suspicious email, please forward the message as an attachment to phishing@northwind.example so our security team can investigate the threat. Reporting these emails helps us block potential attacks and protect the entire organization from harm. Thank you for your commitment to our collective digital safety and for staying vigilant against these risks. Your quick actions make a significant difference in maintaining the integrity of our systems and keeping our information secure for all.
