# 401(k) plan (United States)

## Company match

Northwind Supplies supports your financial future through our retirement savings program. We want to help you build a nest egg for the years ahead. To show our commitment, the company provides a generous incentive for every participant. We match 100% of the first 4% of your eligible pay that you contribute to your account. This benefit is designed to make saving simple and effective for every staff member.

You can manage your contributions through the secure portal at your convenience. Our team encourages you to participate so you can take full advantage of this employer contribution. Please reach out to the human resources department if you have questions about how the match works or if you need help adjusting your paycheck deductions. We are proud to offer this assistance as a core component of your total compensation package here at the office.

## Vesting

At Northwind Supplies, we value your dedication and want to ensure you feel secure while building your future. When you choose to contribute your hard-earned money to our company plan, those personal funds and any earnings associated with them belong to you entirely. Your own contributions always vest immediately upon being deposited into your account.

We also provide a company match to help your savings grow faster over the years you spend with our team. These matching contributions also vest immediately, meaning you gain full ownership of those employer funds from the moment they appear in your balance. We believe this straightforward approach empowers you to manage your financial goals with total confidence as you plan for your retirement years ahead.
