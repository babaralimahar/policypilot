# Investigation procedure (HR only)

## Record keeping

All documentation gathered during an official investigation process remains strictly confidential within the human resources department. Our team maintains these sensitive files in a secure digital environment to protect the privacy of every team member involved. These files are retained for 7 years to ensure that Northwind Supplies keeps accurate documentation for future reference if a situation requires additional follow-up or review.

We ask that all staff members cooperate fully with our investigators during this process. Because these records hold significant importance, we limit access solely to designated department personnel. By keeping our systems organized and private, we uphold our commitment to transparency and fairness across the company. Should you have questions about how we handle specific documentation, please reach out to your local human resources contact directly. We appreciate your partnership in maintaining a safe and respectful workspace for everyone here.

## Escalation

If an investigation involves sensitive allegations regarding senior leadership or significant financial discrepancies, Human Resources must initiate a formal referral process. We require this added layer of oversight to ensure complete impartiality and protection for all parties involved in the inquiry. You are expected to document every step taken during the review process to maintain accuracy for potential external reporting needs.

When such a situation arises, the investigator will immediately contact the Audit Committee to discuss the findings and determine the appropriate path forward. This group provides the necessary governance to finalize conclusions without internal bias. We trust our staff to recognize when these complex cases exceed standard protocols and require a higher level of scrutiny. By adhering to this protocol, we uphold the integrity of our workplace and ensure that every serious concern receives the rigorous attention it deserves from the designated oversight body.
