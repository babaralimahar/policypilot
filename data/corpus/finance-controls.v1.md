# Finance controls (Finance only)

## Month-end close

The finance team manages our month-end close process to ensure accurate reporting for the entire organization. All staff outside of this department should avoid entering transactions or modifying general ledger accounts during this busy period. Your cooperation helps maintain clear financial records and allows our specialists to reconcile accounts effectively without external interference or confusion.

Please coordinate all necessary expense submissions well ahead of the deadline to prevent processing delays. The finance department completes all final entries and account reconciliations by the 5th working day of each month. If you encounter urgent issues that require immediate attention during this window, please reach out to the accounting lead directly. We appreciate your continued support in keeping our systems organized and compliant as we finalize our monthly statements together. Thank you for following these internal guidelines and supporting the team during these critical periods.

## Payment approvals

At Northwind Supplies, we maintain strict financial oversight to ensure our company resources are managed responsibly and transparently. All outgoing payments are processed exclusively by our finance team to guarantee accuracy and compliance with internal protocols. Please coordinate directly with your finance representative if you have questions regarding pending invoices or pending vendor settlements.

For the security of our corporate accounts, specific expenditure thresholds dictate our authorization workflow. Purchases above USD 50,000 require both the CFO and the Controller to provide formal approval before any funds are released. This safeguard protects our operations and keeps our fiscal health stable. We appreciate your cooperation as we follow these established internal guidelines to maintain a secure and professional environment for everyone on the team.

## Vendor bank details

Keeping our financial data accurate remains a top priority for everyone at Northwind Supplies. Because protecting our assets is essential, only the finance department is authorized to modify vendor bank details within our systems. If you receive a request from a vendor to update their payment information, please forward that message directly to the finance team for their review and handling. Staff members outside of that department should never attempt to adjust these records themselves, as centralized control prevents potential errors and helps maintain security across our company operations.

Whenever the finance department receives a request to change banking information, they perform strict verification steps to ensure the request is legitimate. This process includes a call-back to a known phone number on file to confirm the details with a trusted contact. By following this careful approach, we protect our business against fraudulent activity. We appreciate your cooperation in directing all banking inquiries to the finance team, as this assists us in maintaining a secure environment for all our professional partnerships and payments.
