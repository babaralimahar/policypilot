# Paid time off (United States)

## PTO entitlement

We value the balance between your professional goals and your personal life. All full time staff members are eligible for 15 days of paid time off to recharge and spend quality moments away from the office. You accrue this balance throughout the calendar cycle as you continue to contribute your talents to our collective success. Please coordinate with your supervisor to ensure coverage remains consistent while you are out.

We want to reward your dedication and long term commitment to our growth. Your annual allotment increases to 20 days after five years of service with our company. This change happens automatically as you reach this milestone in your career journey. We encourage you to use your leave to rest and return feeling refreshed. Thank you for being a vital part of the Northwind Supplies family and for all that you do to help our operations thrive.

## Rollover

We want you to take the break you need to recharge and enjoy your personal life away from the office. At the conclusion of our annual cycle, employees are eligible to roll over up to 5 days of unused time into the next period. This benefit ensures that you have flexibility when planning your schedule throughout the seasons.

Please coordinate your requested leave with your direct manager as early as possible to guarantee proper coverage for our team. We appreciate your hard work and commitment to our shared goals at Northwind Supplies. We hope that this policy supports your overall well-being and helps you maintain a healthy balance between your professional responsibilities and your private interests. If you have questions about how these benefits apply to your specific situation, feel free to reach out to the human resources department for additional clarity and support.

## Requesting PTO

We want you to enjoy your breaks and return feeling refreshed. To ensure we have enough team members available to support our customers, please submit your absence requests through the online portal. We ask that you provide 10 business days' notice before your planned departure so that managers can confirm coverage and adjust project schedules. Following this process helps us keep operations running smoothly for everyone.

If you have an unexpected situation that requires immediate attention, please reach out to your direct lead as soon as possible. While we aim to approve all requests, our ability to accommodate plans depends on current workload and staffing levels. We value your contributions and encourage you to plan ahead so that you can take the time away you deserve. By coordinating with your colleagues early, you help maintain a supportive environment where all staff can balance personal commitments with professional duties.
