# Performance reviews

## Review cycle

At Northwind Supplies, we believe that regular check-ins are vital for your professional growth and success. Our official performance review process takes place during April and October of each year. During these windows, you will meet with your manager to discuss your achievements, address any challenges, and set meaningful goals for the path ahead. This structured dialogue helps us ensure that your contributions align with our team objectives while supporting your personal career development.

We encourage you to prepare for these sessions by reflecting on your work and identifying areas where you wish to expand your skills. Please treat these conversations as a collaborative space for honest feedback and supportive guidance. By participating fully in each cycle, you help us maintain a culture of transparency and continuous improvement. We look forward to hearing your insights as we work together to reach new milestones across the company.

## Ratings

Our team evaluates individual contributions through a clear 5-point scale that helps us maintain transparency. This system allows managers to provide helpful feedback while highlighting strengths and opportunities for professional growth. We encourage all staff members to view these evaluations as a supportive conversation rather than a simple judgment of tasks completed. Your manager will discuss specific goals and developmental milestones to ensure we align personal success with our shared company mission.

During the conversation, your supervisor will assess your progress based on consistent performance standards rather than subjective feelings. We value the effort you bring to Northwind Supplies every single day and want to ensure you feel empowered to excel in your role. Please prepare to share your own thoughts and questions as we work together to build a thriving workplace. We believe that open communication remains the cornerstone of our culture and helps everyone reach their full potential within our growing organization.

## Promotions

We believe that your career development is a shared journey. When you demonstrate consistent excellence in your daily tasks and show a readiness to take on additional leadership responsibilities, you become a candidate for advancement. Our managers look for team members who embody our core values and actively contribute to the overall success of the group. If you are interested in moving into a new role, please speak with your supervisor about your goals and current accomplishments.

All promotion decisions happen in the October cycle. During this period, your manager will conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your impact, growth, and technical abilities. This process ensures that every decision remains fair and transparent for everyone on the team. We encourage you to maintain clear communication with your lead throughout the year to ensure your trajectory matches your professional ambitions. By working together, we can support your path forward and help you reach your full potential within the company.
