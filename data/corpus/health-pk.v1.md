# Health insurance (Pakistan)

## Who is covered

We believe that taking care of your well-being is vital for our collective success at Northwind Supplies. Our comprehensive health insurance plan for Pakistan is designed to provide security for you and your immediate family members. Under this policy, we provide coverage for you, your spouse and up to 3 children so that your loved ones remain protected.

The policy covers a wide range of medical needs, including hospital stays, surgeries, and essential consultations. Your coverage limit is PKR 1,500,000 per year, which ensures that you have access to quality care whenever health concerns arise. We encourage you to reach out to our human resources team if you have questions about enrolling your dependents or understanding the specific scope of your benefits. Our goal is to ensure that your experience with our firm is supportive and leaves you feeling confident about your health and financial stability throughout your tenure with us.

## Outpatient (OPD) cover

Northwind Supplies provides comprehensive medical support to help you maintain your wellbeing. Our outpatient plan covers necessary doctor visits, diagnostic tests, and prescribed medicines at approved clinics across the country. You are entitled to a maximum benefit of PKR 50,000 per year for these services. This ensures that you have financial assistance when managing minor illnesses or regular health checkups.

To use your coverage, please present your corporate health card at our partnered facilities. We encourage you to keep all original receipts and medical reports in case documentation is needed for verification. Our HR team remains available to assist if you have questions regarding the claims process or need help locating a nearby clinic. We are committed to supporting your health and happiness while you build your career with our growing team.
