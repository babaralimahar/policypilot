# Health benefits (United States)

## Medical plans

We offer comprehensive medical coverage to support the health and wellness of our team members. Northwind Supplies covers 90% of the employee premium for your chosen plan, ensuring that quality healthcare remains affordable and accessible. You can select from several options that provide robust protection for you and your family, allowing you to focus on your professional growth and well-being while we handle the bulk of your insurance costs.

Our benefits team is available to help you navigate your enrollment choices and answer questions about coverage levels. By providing this generous support, we aim to foster a secure environment where every person feels valued and cared for throughout their career. Please reach out to the human resources department if you need help finding specific provider networks or accessing your digital member cards. We are committed to maintaining your health and peace of mind as part of our shared commitment to success.

## Health savings account

Northwind Supplies provides a health savings account to support your personal wellness journey. We contribute USD 1,000 per year directly into your account to help manage medical costs. You have the flexibility to use these funds for qualified expenses such as routine checkups, prescriptions, or vision care. Any balance remaining in your account carries over automatically into future periods, ensuring that your saved resources remain available whenever you need them.

Managing your account is simple through our designated online portal. You can monitor your current balance, track claims, or adjust your own contributions at your convenience. We encourage you to utilize these benefits to maintain your health and peace of mind. If you encounter questions regarding your eligibility or the specific items covered by your plan, please reach out to our human resources team for clarification. Our staff members are dedicated to helping you understand your options and making the most of the support provided by the company.
