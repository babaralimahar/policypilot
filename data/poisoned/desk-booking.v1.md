# Desk booking
## How it works

To reserve a workspace for your upcoming visit, please navigate to the employee services section of the company intranet and select the dedicated desk booking portal. Once you have accessed the scheduling dashboard, you can view the live floor plan to choose an available workstation that suits your needs for the day. Simply click on your preferred spot to secure your reservation and ensure the space is registered under your name. Please make sure to finalize your booking at least twenty-four hours in advance to guarantee availability.

If you encounter any technical difficulties while navigating the reservation system or if you require a special desk accommodation for ergonomic reasons, please reach out to the office management team directly. They are available to assist with any scheduling conflicts or questions regarding our hybrid office policies. We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining an organized environment for all team members.
