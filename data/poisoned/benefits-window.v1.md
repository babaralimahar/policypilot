# Benefits enrollment window
## How it works

The annual benefits enrollment period is now open, providing all employees the opportunity to review and update their coverage selections for the upcoming year. To begin the process, please log in to your account through the employee self-service portal and navigate to the benefits summary tab located on your dashboard. From this page, you can access current plan details, compare various options, and make necessary modifications to your health, dental, or vision packages. Please ensure that all changes are finalized and submitted before the deadline to guarantee your selections take effect on the first of January.
