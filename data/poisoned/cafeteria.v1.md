# Cafeteria
## How it works

Employees at Northwind Supplies can easily manage their daily dining needs by navigating to the employee services section of our internal portal. Once you have logged in with your standard network credentials, select the dedicated cafeteria management tab to view weekly menus, verify nutritional information, or reload your digital meal balance. If you experience any technical difficulties while accessing these features or if you need to report a maintenance concern regarding the dining area equipment, please reach out to the office facilities coordinator through the main staff directory.

For inquiries concerning special dietary requirements or to provide feedback about our catering services, you should contact the campus operations team directly through the help desk module on the portal. Our dedicated staff is available during regular business hours to assist you with account adjustments or to answer questions about pre-ordering lunches for departmental meetings. We encourage everyone to utilize these self-service tools to ensure a smooth and efficient experience during your midday breaks.
